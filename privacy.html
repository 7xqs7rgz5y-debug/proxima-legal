Privacy Policy

Last updated · June 2026

Proxima (“we”, “our”, “the app”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Account — your email address, used only to sign you in with a one-time code.
  • Your content — the tasks, checklists, notes, keywords, and reminders you create.
  • Notifications — if you enable them, a device push token so we can deliver the reminders you schedule.

How we use your information

We use it to provide the app: to store and sync your tasks, and to send the reminders you set. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

Advertising

Proxima does not currently show ads. If we introduce advertising in the future, we will update this policy first. Any advertising would rely only on limited technical data (such as a device advertising identifier) — never the content of your tasks, notes, or reminders — and we will never sell your personal data to advertisers.

Storage & security

Your data is stored with our backend provider (Supabase) and protected by row-level security, so only your authenticated account can access your data. Data is transmitted over encrypted connections.

Retention & deletion

You can permanently delete your account and all associated data at any time from Settings → Delete account. This removes your tasks, reminders, settings, and sign-in record.

Children

Proxima is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We may update this policy; material changes will be reflected here with a new “last updated” date.

Contact

Questions? Contact us at support@proxima.app.
